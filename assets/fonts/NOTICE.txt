Fonts vendored for social-card rendering
========================================

These are the same three families the app itself loads from Google Fonts at
runtime (see src/app/layout.tsx). They are duplicated here as static, subset
files because the social cards are rasterised on the server by Satori, which
needs raw TTF/OTF bytes and cannot read the woff2 that next/font serves to
browsers. Nothing in the browser bundle references this directory.

Each file has been subset to Latin-1 plus the punctuation the cards use
(en/em dash, middle dot, curly quotes, section sign, arrow), which is why they
are ~25KB each rather than ~250KB. Regenerate with:

  python3 -m fontTools.subset <original.ttf> \
    --unicodes="U+0020-007E,U+00A0,U+00A7,U+00B0,U+00B7,U+00D7,U+2010-2015,U+2018-201D,U+2022,U+2026,U+2030,U+2039,U+203A,U+2192,U+00A9,U+00AE,U+2122" \
    --layout-features='kern,liga,tnum' --output-file=<out.ttf>
